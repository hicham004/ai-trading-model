# Tokenized US stocks on OKX — feasibility (plan row R21)

**Status: research only, log-only data.** Nothing here trades, and nothing here is a decision to
trade. The collector (`research/xstocks_data.py`, `scripts/collect_xstocks.py`) reads OKX's public
market-data pages only (no key, no header, no account), the app's instrument allowlist is untouched,
and the design note `docs/design/TOKENIZED_STOCKS.md` lists what would have to change before one of
these pairs is traded even in demo. Written 2026-11-05 on branch `plan/auto`. Spec:
`docs/TRADER_PLAN_2026-11-05.md`, sections 2.8 and 6.2.

What this report rests on: the collector's first live run on 2026-11-05 (universe chosen at
08:11:15 UTC; 1H and daily candles for the 15 pairs back to their listing on 2026-07-16, 42,024
candles, no gaps; one ad-hoc order-book snapshot of every pair at 08:11-08:14 UTC, which is 03:11
New York time, pre-market), the committed universe file `docs/research/xstocks_universe.json`,
the data under `data/lake/xstocks/` (git-ignored) and the per-pair numbers in

## 1. In short

- **The product exists and is reachable the same way as BTC-USDT.** OKX lists 194 SPOT instruments
  in its "Stocks" category (`instCategory` 3): 165 live X-prefixed USDT pairs and 29 USDC-quoted
  twins, all listed between 2026-07-16 and 2026-10-27, all with the same public endpoints, the same
  fee group (12) as BTC-USDT, and parameters the current code can parse. 19 of them exist in OKX's
  demo environment.
- **The money is concentrated.** On 2026-11-05 the 165 USDT pairs traded 141.7 M USDT in 24 hours;
  the top 10 took 79 % of it, 19 pairs traded more than 1 M, 53 less than 10 K, the median pair
  22.5 K. The 15 chosen (section 2) are the top 15 by that measure; the 15th (XTSLA) traded 1.6 M.
- **About half of the weekday volume trades while the US stock market is shut** (43-60 % of weekday
  volume falls in the candles opening 09:00-15:59 New York), and weekends carry 16-36 % of a
  weekday's volume on 14 of the 15 pairs (XSPY's 243 % is three launch-week days in July; since
  September it is 49 %).
- **Weekends move the tokens while the stock cannot trade.** Over 16 weekends the median
  weekend fall below Friday's 16:00 New York close ranged from 0.25 % (XSPY) to 2.3 % (XSOXL, a
  3x leveraged ETF token); in 22 % of the 240 pair-weekends the token fell more than 2.4 % below
  Friday's close at some hourly close (2.4 % is what the plan's 3-ATR stop on BTC 6H costs), in
  15 % more than 3 %, in 5 % more than 5 %. The worst was XSOXL on the weekend of 2026-09-11
  (-17.9 %). The index trackers XSPY and XQQQ never fell more than 1.8 %.
- **Spreads are tight and depth is modest.** In the one pre-market snapshot the median spread was
  2.7 bps (0.8 bps XSPY to 8.0 bps XSOXL) and the depth within 0.5 % of mid per side ranged from
  3.3-3.7 K USDT (XSOXL) to 119-126 K (XCRCL); for a 10-100 USDT demo order every book is deep
  enough, for the plan's live sizes (thousands of USDT) only the top names are.
- **Meaning.** The technical path is open, but the risks the Trader Plan names (section 2.8) are
  visible in the first data: the token keeps moving, sometimes by more than a stop, while the
  stock market is shut; a 3x leveraged ETF token behaves like a crypto coin; and the venue's
  2 %/1 % price bands, the balance "rebasing" for dividends and the Reg S product structure are
  not strategy questions. Collect 60-90 days (the open and close snapshots start with the laptop
  task), then decide at the gate of plan section 6.2.

## 5. Reading against the Trader policy

The Trader Plan's rules (section 4.2) place an ATR stop under every entry: 3 x ATR(14) on the BTC
6H sleeve, which costs about 2.4 % of price, 2 x on the volume-confirmed rules, 4 x on SOL. Nobody
has tuned a rule on these tokens and this report does not propose one; the question the data can
already answer is whether a stop of that size survives a weekend:

- On the index trackers and the largest names (XSPY, XQQQ, XGOOGL, XTSLA, XNVDA) a 2.4 % stop was
  reached on 1 of 80 pair-weekends, and at a cost of at most 3 % below Friday's close. A rule that
  holds these over weekends would mostly be judged by the stock's own moves, which the token
  tracks with a 1-2 % weekend wander.
- On the semiconductor, Korean and crypto-linked names (XINTC, XMU, XSNDK, XSKHY, XMRVL, XAMD,
  XCRCL, XMSTR, XSPCX) a 2.4 % stop was met on 46 of 144 pair-weekends (32 %), with falls up to
  8.3 %. A software stop evaluated on hourly closes would sell into a weekend book that carries a
  fifth of a weekday's volume, at a price that is "last close plus a market estimate" in OKX's own
  words, and the Monday open then re-anchors the token, often the other way (the mean gap is near
  zero). That is the plan's product risk, in numbers: holding these over a weekend with a stop of
  the crypto rules' size is a coin toss on the stop, not on the stock.
- XSOXL is a 3x leveraged ETF token and moves like an altcoin (median weekend deviation 7 %, worst
  18 %); it should not be in any candidate list even though it is the 5th most traded.
- The venue's price bands (2 % at the open, 1 % floating) sit INSIDE the weekend moves measured
  here: an exit that needs more than 1 % from the last price cannot be done in one order. The
  design note treats this as a required change ("price-band-aware orders").

What the data cannot say yet: the spread and depth at the US open and close (the laptop task's
first job; this report has one pre-market snapshot), whether the open-jump pass-through holds on
days with earnings or index events, what the fee rate on these pairs is (not published; the
owner's checklist step 9), who may trade them from the owner's account and from the Singapore VPS
(the same step), and anything about a strategy's return.

## 6. Caveats

- One snapshot per pair so far, outside US hours. Table D will mean something after a few weeks of
  the four daily slots (US open, US close, 03:00 UTC, Saturday 12:00 UTC).
- The weekend measures use hourly closes: the Friday 16:00 close is exact (the 15:00 New York
  candle's close), the "pre-open" price is the 09:00 New York price (half an hour before the open)
  and the "open jump" is the 09:00-10:00 hour, which starts in pre-market. The open-time
  snapshots will give the exact 09:30 book later; the hourly proxies are the best the candles allow.
- The US holiday calendar in the collector (NYSE full-day closures for 2026 and 2027, plus the
  three early closes) was written from the published NYSE calendar on 2026-11-05 and is used only
  to label snapshots and to skip holidays in the weekend arithmetic; no 2026 holiday falls inside
  the 112 days except Labor Day (2026-09-07), which the weekend of 2026-09-04 treats correctly (the
  next trading day is 09-08). Verify the 2027 dates before relying on them (R7 did the same for
  the FOMC calendar); 2028 is not entered yet.
- XSPY's weekend-volume figure is dominated by three launch-week days (above).
- The 24-hour volumes are OKX's own tickers; nothing here checks them against trades.
- Nothing about the demo environment's books is known: the 19 demo pairs' parameters differ from
  the live ones (opening band 10 %), and whether their books are populated is a question for the
  demo validation step of the design note, not for this lake.
